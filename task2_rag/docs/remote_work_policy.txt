Northwind Remote Work Policy (v2.1)

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation may apply for remote work.
Interns are not eligible for remote work during their first 30 days.

2. Remote days
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fridays are
in-office days for all teams unless the department head approves an exception.

3. Equipment
The company provides one laptop and one monitor. Remote employees receive a
one-time home-office stipend of 500 USD, payable after their first month.

4. Security
All remote work must use the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi is not permitted
without the VPN. Lost devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours.
